First release of **Fuseduo**: drop one Mac app onto another and get an automation.

- **Fuse any two apps.** A board of every app on your Mac (Favourites, Recent, categories). Drag one onto another, or press ⌘K and type.
- **Three ideas, or your own.** Claude suggests what the two apps could do together. Ask for more, or describe exactly what you want.
- **Read it before it runs.** Every fusion is a real AppleScript, JavaScript or zsh script, explained step by step in plain English, with a risk rating, the permissions it needs and how to undo it. It's compile-checked and screened against a safety block-list (no deleting, no sudo, no sending messages for you) before the Run button lights up.
- **Refine it.** Type a change ("also include tomorrow") and it rebuilds the script, with one-step undo.
- **Quick script or Mac app.** Run it right here, or turn it into a real app with its own icon in Applications › Fusions that you can double-click or keep in the Dock.
- **Saved fusions.** Keep the ones you like and run them again in one click, with when they last ran and whether it worked.
- **Your Claude, your choice.** Use your own Claude Code sign-in and plan, or an Anthropic API key. Best (Claude Opus 5.5) or Fast (Claude Sonnet 5.5).

**Downloads:** Apple Silicon (`-mac-arm64.dmg`) or Intel (`-mac-x64.dmg`). macOS only for now.
The app isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate: the first time, right-click Fuseduo in Applications and choose **Open**.
